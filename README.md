# Fathom Clone

A rebuild of [fathom.video](https://fathom.video), the AI meeting notetaker, built in one day.

**Live:** https://fathom-clone.fathom-clone.workers.dev. Click **Try the demo account**; no sign-up needed.
**Walkthrough script:** [docs/walkthrough.md](docs/walkthrough.md)

Try a shared clip while signed out: https://fathom-clone.fathom-clone.workers.dev/c/demo-77b76b769e

---

## What it does

**1. The meeting page** is the core of the product, and it got most of the time.
- Video playback synced to the transcript:
  - Click any line to seek.
  - The transcript follows along and highlights who is speaking right now.
  - If you scroll away, follow-along pauses, with a button to resume.
  - `?t=<ms>` deep links open the meeting at an exact moment.
- AI notes with **six switchable templates**: General, Sales, Customer success, 1:1, Standup and Interview. The template that fits each seed meeting is pre-generated. The rest generate the first time you click them and are then cached.
- Every summary bullet has a timestamp you can click to hear where it was said.
- **Action items** carry an owner and a link to the moment they were agreed. You can tick them off.
- Keyboard: space plays and pauses, ←/→ skip 5 seconds.

**2. Meetings list and search across all meetings.** One Postgres full-text query covers titles, every transcript line and every summary. Results are grouped by meeting with highlighted snippets. A transcript hit opens the meeting at that second.

**3. Highlights and shareable clips.**
- Mark a moment from the player ("Highlight" takes ±15s around now, editable) or from the star on any transcript line.
- Highlights show on the meeting timeline and in a list.
- **Share clip** copies a public `/c/<token>` link that works signed out. It plays only the clip window, shows the clip's transcript, and counts views. You can turn the link off again.

**4. The hour-long, 8-person call.** That's the seeded *Q4 Planning* meeting, which is 60 minutes long.
- **Chapters:** AI-detected topics on a coloured timeline (hover to preview, click to jump), plus a chapter list with a one-line gist each.
- **Speakers:** talk time, % share and number of turns per person, plus a lane showing *when* each person spoke. Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to jump through one person's turns, or filter the transcript to just them.
- **A summary that stays good at length:** chapters are found first, and the General notes are then written per chapter. Every bullet cites a transcript line, so you can check it.

**5. Calendar (Google, read-only).** The **Upcoming** page lists the next 14 days of events and detects Meet, Zoom and Teams links.
- The demo account shows a sample week, because it can't connect a real Google account.
- The real OAuth flow is wired up, but the Google app is in *testing* mode, so only listed test users can connect. Opening it to everyone needs Google's verification review for calendar scopes.

**Capture (stubbed, see below).** Under **New recording** you can upload a file or record a browser tab together with your microphone.
- The recording streams to R2 in chunks.
- A resumable pipeline then transcribes it with speaker labels and writes chapters and notes.
- You can rename "Speaker 1" and the others afterwards.

## What's stubbed

- **The meeting bot.** Nothing joins Zoom, Meet or Teams. Capture is an upload, or a recording of a browser tab plus your mic. Everything after capture is real.
- **The seed meetings are scripted.** I wrote six realistic meetings and voiced them with macOS text-to-speech, using a different voice per person. Timestamps come from the audio itself, so playback and transcript line up exactly. The "video" is a generated grid that highlights the active speaker.
  - The summaries, chapters and action items on these meetings were **generated by the real pipeline**, not written by hand.
  - The hour-long call has 8 speakers, about 10,700 words and 477 turns.
- **Sample calendar for the demo account** (see above).

## What I left out, and why

- **Claude for the AI.** I planned it, but I decided to keep this build on free tiers only. The AI runs on **Cloudflare Workers AI**:
  - `gpt-oss-120b` writes chapters and notes. It was the best model available on the free plan when I tested several on the hour-long transcript.
  - Deepgram `nova-3` does speech-to-text with diarization.
  - The free plan has a daily allowance, so templates generate on demand and are cached. If the allowance runs out, the app says so instead of failing silently.
- **Real clip files.** A clip is a time window over the full recording, played inside that window. Someone determined could fetch the whole recording through the signed media URL. Cutting real clip files would mean transcoding, which Workers can't do. With more time I'd cut clips in the browser (ffmpeg.wasm, stream copy) or use Cloudflare Stream.
- **Ask-AI chat over meetings, CRM sync, teams and permissions, and editing the transcript.** These are real Fathom features, but lower value per hour than making the meeting page and the long call excellent.
- **Speaker separation on uploads is approximate.** Diarization merges similar voices; on the TTS test audio it found 3 speakers where there were 5. Renaming speakers is the escape hatch.
- **Uploads are capped at 300 MB.**
- **No pixel-level copy of the real UI.** The planned `/recon` folder (screenshots and notes from my own Fathom test call) wasn't in the repo while this was built. The UI follows Fathom's general shape (player and timeline on the left, notes and transcript on the right) rather than copying it exactly.

## What was tested

Every item below was checked by hand in Chrome against the deployed Worker code with the real database, storage and AI.
- **Playback sync:** a timestamp click seeks the video. The transcript follows along, with the highlighted line and the "X speaking" label matching the lit tile in the video. Clicking a line seeks. Per-speaker next and previous turn jumps work.
- **Notes:** a template generated on demand (Customer success, hour-long call, about 30s). Every bullet and action item links to its moment. Citations are checked against the transcript, and clear misses are re-anchored.
- **Highlights:** created from the player and from a transcript line. Sharing gives a public link that works signed out and plays only the clip window. Turning the link off returns 404. Delete works.
- **Action items** tick off, and **speakers** can be renamed. Both persist across a reload.
- **Search** across meetings, with deep links to the exact second.
- **Capture:** upload through the New recording page, and a real browser-tab recording, each transcribed with chapters and notes.
- **Sign-up** with a personal account.
- **Google Calendar:** connected a real account and a real event showed up on Upcoming with its Meet badge.
- **Demo reset:** meeting URLs and shared-clip links stay the same across resets.
- **Phone-width layout.**

## Architecture

```
Browser (React + Vite + Tailwind)
   │  supabase-js with the user's JWT ──► Supabase Postgres (row level security on every table)
   │                                       - meetings, participants, transcript_segments, chapters,
   │                                         summaries, action_items, highlights, calendar_accounts
   │                                       - search() = full-text search across all three sources
   ▼
One Cloudflare Worker (Hono)  ── serves the SPA and /api/*
   ├─ /api/media/*            R2 streaming with Range support, short-lived HMAC-signed URLs
   ├─ /api/uploads            R2 multipart upload in 8 MB parts
   ├─ /api/meetings/:id/process   resumable pipeline: transcribe → chapters + notes (steps claimed atomically)
   ├─ /api/meetings/:id/summaries/:template   on-demand template generation
   ├─ /api/public/clips/:token   signed-out clip data (service role, scoped to one token)
   ├─ /api/calendar/*         Google OAuth (signed state), events via refresh token kept server-side
   └─ cron 09:00 UTC          rebuilds the shared demo account from a golden copy, deletes visitor uploads
Workers AI: @cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b (notes), @cf/deepgram/nova-3 (speech-to-text + diarization)
```

- `shared/pipeline.ts` is the one AI pipeline. The Worker and the seed script both use it, so seeded meetings went through exactly the code a new upload does.
- **Demo account safety:** everyone shares the demo login, so a nightly cron runs `reset_demo()`. It restores the seeded meetings and highlights, and shifts dates so the newest meeting always reads as "yesterday". Shared seed clips keep stable links.
- **Secrets** are kept only in `.dev.vars` (gitignored) and Worker secrets. The demo password never reaches the browser: the "Try the demo" sign-in happens on the server.

## Running it

```bash
npm install
cp .dev.vars.example .dev.vars   # fill in the Supabase secret key etc.
npm run dev                      # Vite + the Worker locally (R2 and Workers AI are remote bindings)
npm run deploy                   # build + wrangler deploy
```

- **Database:** run `supabase/migrations/*.sql` in order.
- **Seed data:**
  1. `python3 scripts/seed/render.py` renders the audio and video (needs macOS `say` and ffmpeg).
  2. `node scripts/seed/seed.ts` uploads them, runs the AI pipeline and resets the demo account.
