# Walkthrough script (5 minutes, camera on)

Open https://fathom-clone.fathom-clone.workers.dev in a fresh browser window, signed out.

## 0:00–0:30 Intro
- "This is a one-day rebuild of Fathom, the AI meeting notetaker. One Cloudflare Worker, Supabase, R2, and Workers AI."
- "I stubbed the recording bot on purpose. Nothing joins Zoom. You upload a recording or record a browser tab. I spent the time on what happens after the call, because that's the product."
- Click **Try the demo account**.

## 0:30–1:00 Meetings list
- "Six seeded meetings. They're scripted and voiced with text-to-speech, but the notes, chapters and action items were generated by the real pipeline."
- Point at the AI overview line and the action-item and highlight counts.

## 1:00–2:30 The meeting page (Q4 Planning, 60 min, 8 people)
- Open **Q4 Planning**. Press play.
- Switch to **Transcript**: "It follows along and highlights who's talking. Click any line to jump."
- Back to **AI notes**. Click an action item's timestamp, e.g. "Send beta customer criteria to Karen": "Every action item and every bullet links to the moment it was said."
- Switch templates. Click **Sales** (not generated yet): "Other templates generate on demand and are cached."
- Click **General** again and scroll: "On long calls, notes are organised by chapter, so they stay good at length."

## 2:30–3:15 Making an hour-long call usable
- **Chapters** card: "Seven topics, found by the AI. Click one to jump. The coloured timeline shows the same thing."
- **Speakers** card: "Talk time per person, and a lane for when each person spoke."
- Click ▶ next to Rishi a couple of times: "Jump through just Rishi's turns."
- Click the filter icon: "Or read only what Rishi said."

## 3:15–4:00 Highlights and sharing
- Click **Highlight** under the player, type a note, save. It lands on the timeline.
- Click **Share clip**, then open the link in a private window: "Signed out, it plays just that clip with its transcript."

## 4:00–4:30 Search across meetings
- Back to **Meetings** and search **SSO**: "One search across titles, transcripts and summaries, in three meetings."
- Click a transcript hit: "It opens at the exact second."

## 4:30–5:00 What's stubbed and what I left out
- **New recording**: "Upload, or record a tab plus mic. It transcribes with speaker labels, then writes chapters and notes."
- **Upcoming**: "Google Calendar, read-only. The demo shows a sample week. Since there's no bot, the app makes manual capture hard to miss: Join & record opens the call and the recorder in one click, and alerts fire 5 minutes before a meeting and again if it starts while nothing is recording." (The demo has a sample meeting a few minutes out, so the banner appears.)
- "Left out: a real bot, Ask-AI chat, CRM sync, and real clip files (clips are a window over the recording). The README has the reasons."
